Bicameral helps software teams turn scattered product intent, architectural constraints, implementation evidence, and review decisions into governed, traceable work.

The local runtime sits at the team's trust boundary. Integrations and agents can propose evidence and actions, but governance determines what may become canonical.

## Current maturity

Bicameral is an alpha product. The local daemon, gateway, typed protocol, review state, local code grounding, and pluggable event-store interfaces are implemented and tested. Hosted authentication, coordination, installation, and source integrations continue to mature.

<Warning>
  Alpha software should not be treated as a substitute for production backup, identity, change-management, or incident-response practices. Validate the release and deployment mode before using it with sensitive data.
</Warning>

## Product shape

```text
Sources, integrations, users, and agents
                |
                v
      evidence and proposed actions
                |
                v
Bicameral local runtime at the trust boundary
  validation | policy | review | audit | grounding
                |
                v
      approved canonical state
```

The hosted service may provide authentication, coordination, and advisory capabilities. It does not silently inherit local Decision Ledger, customer credential, or policy-root authority.
